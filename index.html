Lumo: Firefly Memory Maze

Look at the garden, then the lights go out. Draw Lumo the firefly's way home from memory.

How to play

Each level shows the garden for a moment. When it gets dark, touch Lumo and draw the way home with your finger. Let go, and Lumo flies along your line. Collect all three drops and fly home in one go without peeking to earn three stars. Tap the eye to peek when you are stuck.

The purchase

Levels 1 to 10 are free. One purchase opens all 6 worlds, 60 levels, the ending party and the golden Lumo, for good and for the whole family with Family Sharing. On a new phone, open the purchase card (the locked gate on the map) and tap "Restore purchase".

Help in the dark

Turn on Dark help in the settings: the phone buzzes and the ring under your finger turns orange when the line gets close to a hedge.
